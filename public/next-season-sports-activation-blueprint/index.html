Selected case studies

Celsius · MLS All-Star Fan Fest

A branded footprint built to own the field at MLS All-Star.

For Celsius at the MLS All-Star Fan Fest in Austin, Rooted built an open-air activation that read from across the park, pulled fans in for sampling and play, and kept its energy from afternoon crowds straight through a packed sunset.

  • Large-format branded structure designed to anchor a busy festival footprint
  • Sampling, photo, and player moments built to keep guests in the space
  • Day-to-night production that stayed photogenic in every light

Celsius · MLS Media Marketing Tour

An Official Energy Drink Partner moment built for media and players.

For the Celsius x MLS Media Marketing Tour, Rooted created an interactive, fully branded environment where players and media could compete, capture content, and feel the partnership, with every wall reinforcing Celsius as the Official Energy Drink Partner of MLS.

  • Custom branded set with an all-club ball wall and partner lockups
  • Interactive foosball moment built for hands-on player and media content
  • Environment engineered to put the Celsius x MLS partnership on camera

Celsius · MLS Cup

An all-club ball wall built to own the biggest day in MLS.

For Celsius at MLS Cup, Rooted built a black, premium activation anchored by an all-club ball wall and the Official Energy Drink Partner lockup, then brought it to life with sampling, athletic moments, and a footprint that pulled match-day fans in from the cold.

  • Custom all-club ball wall designed as a signature photo moment
  • Sampling bar and brand staff built for match-day foot traffic
  • Bold black-and-white build engineered to stand out in a winter setting

Sea-Doo · Social Club

A branded beach club built around the Sea-Doo life.

For the Sea-Doo Social Club Legendary Meetup, Rooted designed a full branded beach footprint, ran on-water demos and games, and turned a day on the sand into a community moment owners actually wanted to be photographed in.

  • Custom branded build, signage, and lounge set against the water
  • On-water riding moments and beach games that kept energy high all day
  • A lifestyle environment made for content, community, and brand love

Stoop Dayz · Hard Soda

A bold beverage footprint built for festivals and game day.

For Stoop Dayz Hard Soda, Rooted built loud, ownable activations that pulled crowds in across both a city festival and a Bengals game day, with branded structures, sampling moments, and games that kept guests in the brand world.

  • High-energy branded build that stood out in a crowded festival
  • Game-day footprint engineered for sampling and fan traffic
  • Hands-on moments and content built to introduce a new soda

Kendo Brands · Fenty Beauty

Retail-inspired beauty environment with a clear content pull.

Rooted helped bring Fenty Beauty into a tactile event environment with strong product visibility, branded set pieces, guided guest moments, and a visual language that felt premium without losing the energy of the room.

  • Dimensional product storytelling and display moments
  • Guest flow built around discovery, sampling, and photo opportunities
  • Brand environment designed to hold up in person and on camera

Kendo Brands · Ole Henriksen

High-color skincare activation built for traffic, trial, and shareability.

The Ole Henriksen footprint leaned into a bold, high-saturation world with product-driven zones, playful details, and a guest experience that could pull people in from the aisle and keep the line moving.

  • Bright, ownable experience zones with clear product education
  • Interactive moments designed for guest participation and capture
  • Production details that turned a busy floor into a branded destination
